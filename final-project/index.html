Welcome to MonoMuse Museum

MonoMuse is a contemporary museum dedicated to exploring the intersection of art, technology, and human creativity. Through immersive exhibitions, interactive installations, and rotating collections, MonoMuse invites visitors to discover how digital innovation is transforming the way we create, experience, and understand art. Whether you are a first-time visitor or a returning member, our exhibitions offer new perspectives on the role of technology in culture and everyday life. Plan your visit to experience cutting-edge exhibitions, hands-on experiences, and events designed to inspire curiosity and creativity.

Find Us

MonoMuse is more than just a gallery—it's a hub for tech-driven artistic exploration. Our visitors often share that seeing code come to life in a visual way changed how they perceive both art and technology.

Beyond the main galleries, we offer workshops on creative coding, AI-generated art, and VR design. Our goal is to bridge the gap between complex engineering and human emotion, providing a unique space where the next generation of creators can find inspiration and community. Whether you're here for a quick tour or a deep dive into our labs, there's always something new to discover at MonoMuse.
